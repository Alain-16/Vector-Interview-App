# VECTOR INTERVIEW SYSTEM

# Background & Context

    Recruiters and hiring managers often struggle with scheduling interviews, managing
    multiple candidates, and maintaining a fair evaluation process. Vector Interview is an
    asynchronous video interview platform designed to streamline the hiring process by allowing
    recruiters to pre-record interview questions and candidates to respond at their convenience.
    The goal is to make hiring more efficient, scalable, and unbiased, ensuring that organizations
    can evaluate talent quickly and fairly while reducing the time spent on manual interviews.

# Business Goals & Objectives

    1.​ Improve Hiring Efficiency – Reduce time spent on scheduling and conducting initial
    interview rounds.
    2.​ Enhance Fairness & Objectivity – Enable independent reviews by multiple evaluators
    to minimize bias.
    3.​ Support Global Hiring – Make interviews accessible to candidates across different
    time zones and locations.
    4.​ Provide Actionable Insights – Automate results compilation and candidate scoring to
    speed up decision-making.
